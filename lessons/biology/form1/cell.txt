BIOLOGY — THE CELL

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit
of living organisms.

Major cell structures include:

• Cell membrane
• Cytoplasm
• Nucleus
• Mitochondria
• Ribosomes

Plant cells also commonly have:
• Cell wall
• Chloroplasts
• Large vacuole
