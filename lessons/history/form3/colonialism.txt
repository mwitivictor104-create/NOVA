HISTORY — COLONIALISM

Colonialism involved political and economic control
of one territory by another power.

In Africa, colonial rule affected:
• Political systems
• Land ownership
• Economies
• Education
• Transport
• Social structures

African communities responded in different ways,
including resistance and adaptation.
