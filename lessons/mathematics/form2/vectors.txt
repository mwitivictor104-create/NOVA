MATHEMATICS — FORM 2
TOPIC: VECTORS

A vector has both magnitude and direction.

A scalar has magnitude only.

Examples of vectors:
• Displacement
• Velocity
• Force

Examples of scalars:
• Distance
• Speed
• Mass
• Time

Vectors can be represented using arrows.

PRACTICE

1. Define a vector.
2. Define a scalar.
3. Give two examples of each.
