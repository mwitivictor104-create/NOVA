MATHEMATICS — FORM 2
TOPIC: INDICES

An index represents repeated multiplication.

2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8

Important laws:

aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ

aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ

(aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ

a⁰ = 1, where a is not zero.

Example:

2³ × 2²
= 2⁵
= 32

PRACTICE

1. Simplify 3² × 3³.
2. Simplify 5⁶ ÷ 5².
3. Evaluate 10⁰.
4. Evaluate 2⁴.
