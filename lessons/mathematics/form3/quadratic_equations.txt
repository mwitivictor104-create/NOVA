MATHEMATICS — FORM 3
TOPIC: QUADRATIC EQUATIONS

A quadratic equation commonly has the form:

ax² + bx + c = 0

where a is not zero.

Example:

x² - 5x + 6 = 0

Factorize:

(x - 2)(x - 3) = 0

Therefore:

x = 2 or x = 3

The quadratic formula is:

x = (-b ± √(b² - 4ac)) / 2a

Solve:

1. x² - 7x + 12 = 0
2. x² - 9 = 0
3. x² + 5x + 6 = 0
