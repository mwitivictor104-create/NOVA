MATHEMATICS — FORM 1
TOPIC: ALGEBRA

Algebra uses letters and symbols to represent unknown values.

Example:

x + 5 = 12

Subtract 5 from both sides:

x = 7

Terms:
• Variable
• Constant
• Coefficient
• Expression
• Equation

Example:

3x + 4

3 is the coefficient.
x is the variable.
4 is the constant.

PRACTICE

1. Solve x + 8 = 15.
2. Solve x - 4 = 9.
3. Solve 2x = 18.
4. Simplify 3x + 2x.
