MATHEMATICS — FORM 4
TOPIC: CALCULUS

Calculus studies change and accumulation.

Two major branches are:

1. Differentiation
2. Integration

Differentiation is commonly used to find rates of change.

Integration can be used to find accumulated quantities
and areas.

Example:

If:

y = x²

then:

dy/dx = 2x

PRACTICE

Differentiate:

1. y = x³
2. y = 5x²
3. y = 7x
