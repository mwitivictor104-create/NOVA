PHYSICS — ELECTRICITY

Electric current is the flow of electric charge.

Important quantities:

Current — measured in amperes (A)
Voltage — measured in volts (V)
Resistance — measured in ohms (Ω)

Ohm's law:

V = IR

where:
V = voltage
I = current
R = resistance

PRACTICE

1. State Ohm's law.
2. If V = 12 V and R = 4 Ω, find I.
