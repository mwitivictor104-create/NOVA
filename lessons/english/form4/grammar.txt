TITLE
English Form 4 Grammar

OBJECTIVES
- Understand important English grammar rules.
- Identify correct grammatical structures.
- Improve sentence construction.
- Prepare for examination grammar questions.

LESSON
Grammar is the system of rules used to form correct sentences.

1. Parts of Speech
The major parts of speech include nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions and interjections.

2. Tenses
English uses different tenses to show when an action happens.

Examples:
Present: She reads a book.
Past: She read a book.
Future: She will read a book.

3. Subject-Verb Agreement
The subject and verb must agree in number.

Example:
He plays football.
They play football.

4. Active and Passive Voice
Active voice focuses on the person performing an action.

Example:
The student wrote the answer.

Passive voice focuses on the action or receiver.

Example:
The answer was written by the student.

5. Direct and Indirect Speech
Direct speech gives the exact words spoken.

Example:
John said, "I am ready."

Indirect speech reports what someone said.

Example:
John said that he was ready.

6. Conditional Sentences
Conditional sentences describe possible or imaginary situations.

Example:
If it rains, we will stay at home.

7. Question Tags
A question tag is a short question added to a statement.

Example:
You are coming, aren't you?

KEY POINTS
- Grammar helps us construct correct sentences.
- Verbs change according to tense and subject.
- Subject-verb agreement is important.
- Active and passive voice have different structures.
- Direct speech gives the speaker's exact words.
- Indirect speech reports the speaker's message.

PRACTICE
1. What is grammar?
2. Give one example of a present tense sentence.
3. What is subject-verb agreement?
4. Change "The teacher marked the book" into passive voice.
5. Change "Mary said, I am happy" into indirect speech.

ANSWERS
1. Grammar is the system of rules used to form correct sentences.
2. She reads a book.
3. It means that the subject and verb must agree in number.
4. The book was marked by the teacher.
5. Mary said that she was happy.
